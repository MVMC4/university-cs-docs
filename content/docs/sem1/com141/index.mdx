# COM141: Communication and Academic Literacy Skills

COM141 develops the communication and information-literacy habits required for successful study in the sciences. The teaching plan moves from university learning and communication contexts through listening, speaking, reading, writing, source evaluation, and ethical use of information.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of the course, students should be able to:

1. Communicate effectively through speaking, writing, reading, and listening in science contexts.
2. Apply communication principles in academic and social situations.
3. Search for, evaluate, and ethically use information sources.
4. Read, interpret, critique, and write academic texts appropriate to a scientific discipline.
5. Participate in discussions and deliver structured oral presentations.

## Syllabus Map

- University learning, study habits, time management, goal setting, and library orientation
- Communication processes, modes, barriers, and academic contexts
- Listening, note-taking, speaking, presentation planning, and feedback
- Reading for academic purposes, study-reading strategies, and interpreting graphics
- Evaluating sources for currency, authority, relevance, accuracy, objectivity, and evidence
- Note-making, paraphrasing, summarising, plagiarism, copyright, citation, and referencing
