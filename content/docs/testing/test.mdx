## 5. Math Environments (All Variations)

<MathEnv type="definition" number="1.1" title="Probability Mass Function">
  For a discrete random variable $X$, the PMF $p(x)$ is defined as $p(x) = P(X = x)$. A valid PMF must satisfy $p(x) \\ge 0$ and $\\sum p(x) = 1$.
</MathEnv>

<MathEnv type="theorem" number="1.2" title="The Memoryless Property">
  The Exponential distribution is the *unique* continuous distribution possessing the memoryless property: $P(X \\gt s + t \\mid X \\gt s) = P(X \\gt t)$.
</MathEnv>

<MathEnv type="lemma" number="1.3" title="Geometric Series Sum">
  For $|r| < 1$, the infinite geometric series converges: $\\sum_{k=0}^{\\infty} ar^k = \\frac{a}{1-r}$.
</MathEnv>

<MathEnv type="corollary" number="1.4" title="Geometric Survival">
  Following from Lemma 1.3, the survival function of a Geometric RV is $P(X \\ge x) = q^{x-1}$.
</MathEnv>

<MathEnv type="example" number="1.5" title="Coin Tossing">
  If a fair coin is tossed 10 times, the probability of exactly 3 heads is modeled by $X \\sim \\text{Bin}(10, 0.5)$.
</MathEnv>

## 6. Equation Breakdown & Case Analysis

<EquationBreakdown>
  <Equation>
    $$ P(X=x) = \\frac{\\binom{M}{x}\\binom{N-M}{n-x}}{\\binom{N}{n}} $$
  </Equation>
  <TermGrid>
    <Term symbol={"$N$"} meaning="Total population size" domain="N \\in \\mathbb{Z}^+" />
    <Term symbol={"$M$"} meaning="Total successes in population" domain="0 \\le M \\le N" />
    <Term symbol={"$n$"} meaning="Sample size drawn" domain="0 \\le n \\le N" />
    <Term symbol={"$x$"} meaning="Observed successes" domain="\\max(0, n-(N-M)) \\le x \\le \\min(n, M)" />
  </TermGrid>
</EquationBreakdown>

<CaseAnalysis title="Evaluating Absolute Value in Variance">
  <Case condition={"$X \\ge \\mu$"} explanation="The outcome is greater than or equal to the mean.">
    $$ |X - \\mu| = X - \\mu $$
  </Case>
  <Case condition={"$X \\lt \\mu$"} explanation="The outcome is strictly less than the mean, requiring a sign flip.">
    $$ |X - \\mu| = -(X - \\mu) = \\mu - X $$
  </Case>
</CaseAnalysis>
