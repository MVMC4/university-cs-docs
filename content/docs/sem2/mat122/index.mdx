# MAT122: Introductory Mathematics II

MAT122 continues MAT111 with the core ideas and techniques of calculus, then applies them to areas, volumes, and coordinate geometry.

## Learning Outcomes

1. Demonstrate understanding of basic calculus ideas and techniques.
2. Find standard forms of lines and distances from points to lines.
3. Solve mathematical problems using clear symbolic and graphical reasoning.

## Syllabus Map

- Limits and continuity
- Differentiation and its rules
- Applications of differentiation: extrema, monotonicity, concavity, and curve sketching
- Integration: antiderivatives, definite integrals, substitution, exponentials, parts, and partial fractions
- Applications of integration: areas and volumes of revolution
- Coordinate geometry: standard equations of lines and point-to-line distance
