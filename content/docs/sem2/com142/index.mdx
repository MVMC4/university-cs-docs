# COM142: Academic Writing

COM142 extends communication skills into academic writing. The Semester 2 notes emphasise writing as a process and as a way of learning: develop a position, support it with evidence, and revise for a reader.

## Syllabus Map

- Academic writing, audience, purpose, formality, and style
- The writing process: prewriting, drafting, revising, editing, and proofreading
- Paragraph structure, cohesion, transitions, and essay organisation
- Argument, evidence, counterargument, and cautious academic claims
- Research, paraphrasing, summarising, citation, and avoiding plagiarism
- Group research and presentation tasks

The available study interface, essay sample, and group-project source pack are collected in **Resources**.
