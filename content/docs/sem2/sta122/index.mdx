# STA122: Introductory Concepts of Probability

STA122 develops a working understanding of probability and statistical distributions with emphasis on applications to real-world problems.

## Learning Outcomes

Students should be able to model events, calculate probabilities, work with random variables, and choose appropriate probability distributions.

## Syllabus Map

- Set theory, Venn diagrams, and algebra of sets
- Sample spaces, events, axioms, propositions, and combinatorial probability
- Conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, independence, and tree diagrams
- Discrete and continuous random variables, distribution functions, expectation, and variance
- Uniform, Bernoulli, binomial, geometric, negative binomial, and Poisson distributions
- Continuous uniform, exponential, and normal distributions and their applications
