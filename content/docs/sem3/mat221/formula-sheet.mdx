# MAT221 Formula Sheet

<Callout title="Use formulas with conditions">
A formula is not a method decision. Check its hypotheses, identify the matching structure, and verify the result.
</Callout>

<Mat221FormulaSheet title="Substitution">

$$
\int f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx=\int f(u)\,du,\qquad u=g(x).
$$

For definite integrals, replace the bounds by $g(a)$ and $g(b)$ and remain in $u$.

</Mat221FormulaSheet>

<Mat221FormulaSheet title="Integration by parts">

$$
\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du.
$$

Choose the differentiating factor so it becomes simpler and the remaining integral is genuinely easier.

</Mat221FormulaSheet>

<Mat221FormulaSheet title="Trigonometric identities">

$$
\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1,qquad 1+\tan^2x=\sec^2x.
$$

$$
\sin^2x=\frac{1-\cos2x}{2},\qquad
\cos^2x=\frac{1+\cos2x}{2}.
$$

</Mat221FormulaSheet>

<Mat221FormulaSheet title="Improper integrals">

Replace each infinite endpoint or discontinuity by a one-sided limit. Split at every interior singularity; all resulting limits must converge.

</Mat221FormulaSheet>

<Mat221FormulaSheet title="Power and Taylor series">

$$
f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}a_n(x-c)^n,qquad
f(x)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{f^{(n)}(c)}{n!}(x-c)^n.
$$

Find the radius first, then test both endpoints independently.

</Mat221FormulaSheet>
