## The decision process

1. If $\deg P\ge\deg Q$, perform polynomial division first.
2. Factor $Q(x)$ completely over the real numbers.
3. Write the correct template for every factor and every repeated power.
4. Clear denominators and solve for the constants.
5. Integrate each term.

## The four templates

| Denominator factor | Terms to include |
| --- | --- |
| distinct linear $(x-a)$ | $\dfrac{A}{x-a}$ |
| repeated linear $(x-a)^m$ | $\dfrac{A_1}{x-a}+\dfrac{A_2}{(x-a)^2}+\cdots+\dfrac{A_m}{(x-a)^m}$ |
| irreducible quadratic $q(x)$ | $\dfrac{Bx+C}{q(x)}$ |
| repeated quadratic $q(x)^m$ | one linear numerator over every power $q,q^2,\ldots,q^m$ |

<Callout label="Why Bx+C?">The numerator over a quadratic must have degree less than $2$, so it is the most general linear expression.</Callout>

## Worked example: repeated linear factor

Evaluate

$$
\int\frac{x+3}{x(x-1)^2}\,dx.
$$

The denominator has $x$ and the repeated factor $(x-1)^2$, so write

$$
\frac{x+3}{x(x-1)^2}=\frac{A}{x}+\frac{B}{x-1}+\frac{C}{(x-1)^2}.
$$

Clear denominators:

$$
x+3=A(x-1)^2+Bx(x-1)+Cx.
$$

Use convenient values first:

$$
x=0:\ A=3,\qquad x=1:\ C=4.
$$

Comparing the $x^2$ coefficients gives $A+B=0$, so $B=-3$. Hence

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int\frac{x+3}{x(x-1)^2}\,dx
&=\int\left(\frac3x-\frac3{x-1}+\frac4{(x-1)^2}\right)dx\\
&=\boxed{3\ln|x|-3\ln|x-1|-\frac4{x-1}+C}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Check the decomposition before integrating by recombining the fractions.

## Irreducible quadratics

For $\int dx/(x^2+2x+5)$, complete the square:

$$
x^2+2x+5=(x+1)^2+4.
$$

Using the arctangent form,

$$
\boxed{\int\frac{dx}{x^2+2x+5}=\frac12\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{x+1}{2}\right)+C}.
$$

<Callout label="Common error" tone="trap">For $(x-a)^3$, include denominators $(x-a)$, $(x-a)^2$, and $(x-a)^3$. A missing power makes the decomposition wrong.</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- Decomposing before making an improper rational function proper.
- Omitting intermediate powers of a repeated factor.
- Using a constant numerator over an irreducible quadratic.
- Solving coefficients correctly but forgetting to integrate every term.
