# MAT221: Calculus II

MAT221 develops a connected method library for integration, applications, sequences, and series. The central question is not only *how* to carry out a technique, but *how to recognize which technique is justified*.

<Callout title="Migration status">
The Integration by Substitution chapter is the reviewed architecture pilot. The remaining fourteen chapters stay out of active navigation until their authored questions, review, and exam material pass the same content gate.
</Callout>

## Course sequence

1. Antiderivatives and the Fundamental Theorem
2. Integration by Substitution
3. Partial Fractions
4. Integration by Parts
5. Reduction Formulas
6. Trigonometric Integrals
7. Trigonometric Substitution
8. Weierstrass Substitution
9. Improper Integrals
10. Numerical Integration
11. Applications of Integration
12. L’Hôpital’s Rule
13. Sequences and Series
14. Power Series
15. Taylor Series

## How to study each chapter

Follow the same route every time:

1. read the chapter orientation;
2. work through the complete notes and examples;
3. solve the written questions without opening solutions;
4. use flashcards before the explained quiz;
5. finish with timed exam practice.
