## 4.1 Graph Traversals & Pathfinding

Traversing a graph requires systematically visiting every vertex and edge. The choice of traversal algorithm dictates whether we explore broadly (level-by-level) or deeply (branch-by-branch).

<HighlightCard
  variant="neutral"
  title="Definition 4.1.1: Level-Order (BFS) vs. Deep-Branch (DFS)"
  description="The two fundamental graph exploration paradigms."
>

- **Breadth-First Search (BFS):** Utilizes a **Queue** (FIFO). Explores all immediate neighbors before moving to the next depth level. **Guarantees the shortest path** in unweighted graphs.
- **Depth-First Search (DFS):** Utilizes a **Stack** (LIFO) or recursion. Explores as deep as possible down a single branch before backtracking.

</HighlightCard>

<HighlightCard
  variant="neutral"
  title="Theorem 4.1.1: Dijkstra's Greedy Choice & Relaxation"
  description="Finding the shortest path in weighted graphs with non-negative edges."
>

Dijkstra's Algorithm uses a **Priority Queue** (Min-Heap) to greedily extract the unvisited node with the smallest known distance.
**Edge Relaxation:** If `dist[u] + weight(u, v) < dist[v]`, update `dist[v]` and push it to the Priority Queue.

</HighlightCard>

<StepByStep>
  <StepByStepTitle>Exam Scenario: Tracing Dijkstra's on a 5-Node Graph</StepByStepTitle>

  <Step number={1}>
    <StepTitle>The Graph Setup</StepTitle>
    Nodes: A, B, C, D, E.
    Edges: A-B(4), A-C(2), C-B(1), B-D(5), C-D(8), D-E(2), C-E(10).
    Find shortest path from **A** to all nodes.
  </Step>

  <Step number={2}>
    <StepTitle>Initialization</StepTitle>
    `dist[A]=0`, all others $\infty$. PQ: `[(A, 0)]`.
  </Step>

  <Step number={3}>
    <StepTitle>Extract A (dist 0)</StepTitle>
    Relax neighbors: B gets 4, C gets 2.
    PQ: `[(C, 2), (B, 4)]`.
  </Step>

  <Step number={4}>
    <StepTitle>Extract C (dist 2)</StepTitle>
    Relax neighbors: B gets `2+1=3` (Update B from 4 to 3!). D gets `2+8=10`. E gets `2+10=12`.
    PQ: `[(B, 3), (B, 4), (D, 10), (E, 12)]`.
  </Step>

  <Step number={5}>
    <StepTitle>Extract B (dist 3)</StepTitle>
    *(Note: The `(B, 4)` entry is stale and will be ignored when popped later).*
    Relax neighbors: D gets `3+5=8` (Update D from 10 to 8!).
    PQ: `[(B, 4), (D, 8), (D, 10), (E, 12)]`.
  </Step>

  <Step number={6}>
    <StepTitle>Extract B (dist 4) -> STALE, Ignore.</StepTitle>
    Extract D (dist 8). Relax neighbors: E gets `8+2=10` (Update E from 12 to 10!).
    PQ: `[(D, 10), (E, 10), (E, 12)]`.
  </Step>

  <Step number={7}>
    <StepTitle>Final Distances</StepTitle>
    A:0, B:3, C:2, D:8, E:10. Shortest path A to E is 10 (A -> C -> B -> D -> E).
  </Step>
</StepByStep>
