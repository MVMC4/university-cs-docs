# Trees and Graphs

Non-linear data structures are used to represent hierarchical or networked relationships between data points.

## Binary Search Trees (BST)
A tree where each node has at most two children, and the left child is less than the parent, while the right child is greater.
- **Search**: $O(\log n)$ average, $O(n)$ worst case (unbalanced).
- **Insertion/Deletion**: $O(\log n)$ average.

## Graphs
A collection of nodes (vertices) and edges connecting them. Can be directed or undirected, weighted or unweighted.
- **Traversal**: Depth-First Search (DFS) or Breadth-First Search (BFS).
- **Use Cases**: Social networks, GPS routing, dependency resolution.
