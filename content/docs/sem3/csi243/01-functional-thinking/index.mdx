## Chapter 1: Programming and Functional Thinking

This chapter builds the mental model used throughout CSI243. Complete the notes in order.

| Order | Lesson | Main question |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | [Programming and the Functional Model](/docs/sem3/csi243/01-functional-thinking/notes/programming-model) | How do input, processing, output, storage, values, and functions fit together? |

After the notes, use [Practice Questions](/docs/sem3/csi243/01-functional-thinking/questions) for written application and [Review](/docs/sem3/csi243/01-functional-thinking/review) for flashcards and the chapter quiz.

## Chapter outcomes

- Explain programming using input, processing, output, and storage.
- Distinguish values, expressions, functions, and effects.
- Explain purity, immutability, and composition.
- Compare functional and imperative problem-solving styles.
- Separate a pure calculation from the effectful boundary around it.
