## Decoding the questions

Lecturers and lab sheets often describe a function in language that is precise but far denser than the function itself. The gap between "I don't understand this question" and "I don't understand Haskell" is frequently just that: **the English needs decoding before the Haskell can start.** This page teaches that decoding as its own skill, worked through real CSI243 questions, so the method transfers to a question you have never seen before — including in an exam.
