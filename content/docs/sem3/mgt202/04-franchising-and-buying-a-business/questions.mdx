<ExamQuestion title="Question 1: Franchising and its terms [11 marks]">
  <SubQuestion
    part="a"
    prompt={<>Define franchising and distinguish the franchisor from the franchisee. [4]</>}
    solution={<><ul><li>A business arrangement in which an owner grants another entrepreneur permission to use its brand and specified business methods under agreed conditions [2]</li><li>The franchisor grants the rights; the franchisee invests in and operates the franchised business under the agreement [2]</li></ul></>}
  />
  <SubQuestion
    part="b"
    prompt={<>Distinguish product and trade-name franchising from business-format franchising. [4]</>}
    solution={<><ul><li>Product and trade-name: permission to sell specified products under the franchisor's name or trademark, usually with less control over the whole operation [2]</li><li>Business-format: a broader system including brand identity, operating procedures, training, marketing support, service standards, purchasing and reporting [2]</li></ul></>}
  />
  <SubQuestion
    part="c"
    prompt={<>Explain why the initial franchise fee is not the whole investment. [3]</>}
    solution={<><ul><li>The fee is only one part of the investment [1]</li><li>Premises, equipment and inventory may also be needed, with staff [1]</li><li>Working capital is needed to cover the early months [1]</li></ul></>}
  />
</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion title="Question 2: Franchise costs [10 marks]">
  <SubQuestion
    part="a"
    prompt={<>A franchise has monthly revenue of P80,000, pays a 5% royalty and a 2% advertising contribution. Calculate each payment. [4]</>}
    solution={<><ul><li>Royalty: P80,000 x 5% = P4,000 [2]</li><li>Advertising contribution: P80,000 x 2% = P1,600 [2]</li></ul></>}
  />
  <SubQuestion
    part="b"
    prompt={<>Calculate the revenue that remains after both payments. [2]</>}
    solution={<><ul><li>Combined payments: P4,000 + P1,600 = P5,600 [1]</li><li>Remaining revenue: P80,000 - P5,600 = P74,400 [1]</li></ul></>}
  />
  <SubQuestion
    part="c"
    prompt={<>Explain why the answer to (b) is not the franchisee's profit, and why a revenue-based royalty is a risk. [4]</>}
    solution={<><ul><li>It is not profit because stock or materials, wages, rent, utilities and other expenses must still be paid from it [2]</li><li>The royalty is due on revenue even when the outlet makes little profit or a loss [2]</li></ul></>}
  />
</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion title="Question 3: Evaluating a franchise [13 marks]">
  <SubQuestion
    part="a"
    prompt={<>List the seven steps for evaluating a franchise opportunity. [7]</>}
    solution={<><ul><li>Assess personal suitability [1]</li><li>Investigate the market [1]</li><li>Investigate the franchisor [1]</li><li>Speak to existing and former franchisees [1]</li><li>Examine the financial requirements [1]</li><li>Review the agreement and documents [1]</li><li>Obtain independent review and decide [1]</li></ul></>}
  />
  <SubQuestion
    part="b"
    prompt={<>Explain why a prospective franchisee should speak to former franchisees as well as current ones. [3]</>}
    solution={<><ul><li>Former franchisees can explain why they left and what difficulties occurred [1]</li><li>One current franchisee may give a favourable or incomplete account, so several sources should be used [1]</li><li>Their answers can be compared with the franchisor's claims about costs, training, support and time to stable cash flow [1]</li></ul></>}
  />
  <SubQuestion
    part="c"
    prompt={<>Describe three things to check in the franchise agreement and documents. [3]</>}
    solution={<>Any three of: territory; suppliers; fees; quality standards; renewal; termination and sale; and the franchise disclosure document, which covers costs, restrictions and risks. [3]</>}
  />
</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion title="Question 4: Buying an existing business [15 marks]">
  <SubQuestion
    part="a"
    prompt={<>Give four reasons for buying an existing business. [4]</>}
    solution={<><ul><li>Reducing uncertainties, because an operating history gives records of sales, costs and customer behaviour [1]</li><li>Acquiring ongoing operations and relationships [1]</li><li>A favourable price [1]</li><li>Entering the market more quickly [1]</li></ul></>}
  />
  <SubQuestion
    part="b"
    prompt={<>Explain why a buyer should examine financial records over several years, and name two warning signs. [4]</>}
    solution={<><ul><li>Several years (the slides say three to five) show trends rather than a single year [2]</li><li>Two warning signs such as sales not supported by evidence, a customer holding more than 20% of sales, profit that does not turn into cash, or customers not paying on time [2]</li></ul></>}
  />
  <SubQuestion
    part="c"
    prompt={<>A business has relevant assets with a fair value of P500,000 and liabilities of P180,000. Calculate its net asset value and give one limitation of the asset-based approach. [3]</>}
    solution={<><ul><li>Net asset value: P500,000 - P180,000 = P320,000 [2]</li><li>It may overlook earning capacity and goodwill [1]</li></ul></>}
  />
  <SubQuestion
    part="d"
    prompt={<>State the four closing steps listed in the class slides. [4]</>}
    solution={<><ul><li>A bill of sale transfers the assets [1]</li><li>Tax clearances confirm no tax obligations pass to the buyer [1]</li><li>Lease assignment, with the landlord's consent [1]</li><li>Payment escrow holds the funds until conditions are met [1]</li></ul></>}
  />
</ExamQuestion>
