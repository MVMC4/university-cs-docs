## MGT202 Small Business Management

This course follows the lecturer's notes and class decks, taught by the Department of Entrepreneurship and Business Development at the University of Botswana. It asks how a small business becomes a worthwhile opportunity, how it is run with integrity, and how an entrepreneur decides which route into business to take and whether an idea is feasible.

Each topic is written from the lecturer's own material first. Where a point comes only from the textbook (Longenecker and others, *Small Business Management*, 2nd South African edition) or from an older archived deck, the page says so.

## Course outcomes

By the end of the topics released so far, students should be able to:

- explain why small business and entrepreneurship matter to Botswana, and tell a small business from an entrepreneurial opportunity;
- explain integrity, apply an ethical decision-making process, and describe the responsibilities of a small business;
- define a customer problem precisely before designing a solution;
- compare starting a business, operating a franchise and buying an existing business;
- describe how a family business is structured, governed and handed on;
- run a feasibility analysis across the product, market, organization and finances of an idea.
