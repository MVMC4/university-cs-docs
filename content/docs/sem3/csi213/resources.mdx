## Standard Normal (Z) Table Protocols

*   **Table Navigation:** Rows denote the Z-score to the first decimal place; columns denote the second decimal place. The intersection yields the cumulative probability $P(Z < z)$.
*   **Symmetry Axiom:** For negative Z-scores, apply the symmetry property: $P(Z < -z) = 1 - P(Z < z)$.
*   **Critical Values:** Memorize standard confidence interval thresholds:
    *   $Z_{0.05} = -1.645$ (90% Confidence Interval)
    *   $Z_{0.025} = -1.96$ (95% Confidence Interval)

## Computational Tools

For scientific calculators (e.g., Casio fx-991EX ClassWiz):
*   **Combinatorics:** Utilize the `nCr` and `nPr` functions located within the `OPTN` probability menu.
*   **Statistical Modes:** Engage the `STAT` mode to input raw datasets, enabling automated computation of $\bar{x}$, $\sigma$, and summation terms required for regression analysis.

## Resources Summary

The tools and references compiled in this section are designed to streamline the computational burden of statistical analysis. Mastery of the Standard Normal Table and the statistical modes of scientific calculators will significantly reduce calculation time during term tests and the final examination, allowing students to focus on methodological accuracy and logical deduction.
