## Aims and Learning Objectives
The course aims to teach the basic concepts and principles of probability and discrete mathematics. Students will learn to apply these mathematical concepts to solve complex computational and real-world problems, focusing on counting techniques, probabilistic modeling, random variables, statistical distributions, and regression analysis.

## Learning Outcomes
Upon successful completion of the course, students should be able to:
1. Apply combinatorial principles (permutations and combinations) to count finite sample spaces.
2. Understand and apply the axiomatic foundations of probability theory.
3. Calculate conditional probabilities and analyze the independence and reliability of systems.
4. Model real-world phenomena using discrete and continuous probability distributions.
5. Apply regression analysis to understand relationships between variables and make predictions.

## Documentation Index & Syllabus Mapping
This index maps the official course topics to the specific chapters in the documentation.

### 01. Set Theory & Combinatorics
- **Syllabus Topic:** Counting – permutations and combinations.
- **Documentation Focus:** Set algebra, summation notation, the multiplication principle, permutations, combinations, and discrete counting techniques required for finite probability spaces.

### 02. Axiomatic Probability
- **Syllabus Topic:** Introduction to Probability.
- **Documentation Focus:** Sample spaces, events, classical probability, and the rigorous Kolmogorov axioms (Non-negativity, Normalization, Additivity) with formal theorem proofs.

### 03. Conditional Probability & Independence
- **Syllabus Topic:** Conditional probability, Independence, reliability.
- **Documentation Focus:** Bayes' Theorem, the Law of Total Probability, independent events, and applying probability to system reliability models.

### 04. Random Variables & Variance
- **Syllabus Topic:** Introduction to Distributions.
- **Documentation Focus:** Formal definition of random variables ($X: S \to \mathbb{R}$), PMFs, PDFs, CDFs, expected value, and variance computational proofs.

### 05. Discrete Probability Distributions
- **Syllabus Topic:** Distributions – geometric, binomial, Poisson, hypergeometric.
- **Documentation Focus:** Bernoulli trials, Binomial distribution, Poisson approximation, Geometric distribution, and Hypergeometric distribution with their respective means and variances.

### 06. Continuous Probability Distributions
- **Syllabus Topic:** Distributions – exponential, normal.
- **Documentation Focus:** Continuous random variables, the Normal (Gaussian) distribution, Standard Normal tables, the Exponential distribution, and their applications in modeling time and physical phenomena.

### 07. Regression Analysis
- **Syllabus Topic:** Regression analysis.
- **Documentation Focus:** Simple linear regression, the least squares method, correlation coefficients, and interpreting regression models for predictive analysis.
