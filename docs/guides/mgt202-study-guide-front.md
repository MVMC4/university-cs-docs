This guide is built from the MGT202 pages on University CS Docs, so it matches the site. It covers Topics 1 to 5 and 7. Topic 6 is missing because no lecturer material has been issued for it; it will be added when it is.

### How each topic is laid out

1. **Notes**: the full notes, with the definitions, tables, worked examples and common mistakes.
2. **Key ideas**: the topic's flashcards as a table. Cover the right-hand column and test yourself.
3. **Practice questions**: questions with marks. The worked solutions show how an examiner would award the marks.
4. **Quiz bank**: multiple-choice questions with the answer and the reason for it.

On screen, answers and solutions are folded away, so try each one first. In the PDF they are all open.

### How sources are handled

- The lecturer's notes and class decks come first. A point that comes only from the textbook (Longenecker and others, *Small Business Management*, 2nd South African edition) or from an older archived deck is marked as such.
- Where the student notes, the slides and the textbook word something differently, the notes say so and tell you which version to use.
- The textbook's statistics are South African. Do not present them as Botswana statistics.
- Business examples, prices and survey results in Topic 7 are fictional teaching material. Every calculation in it was recomputed.
- Case facts and the lecturer's class examples are historical illustrations, not current figures.
