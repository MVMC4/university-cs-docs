# MAT221: Calculus I

Calculus is the mathematical study of continuous change. This course covers the foundational concepts of limits, derivatives, and introductory integrals, which are essential for physics, engineering, and advanced computer science (like machine learning).

## Core Areas
1. **Limits and Continuity**: The foundation of calculus.
2. **Derivatives**: Rates of change and slopes of tangent lines.
3. **Applications of Derivatives**: Optimization, related rates, and curve sketching.
4. **Integrals**: Area under a curve and anti-derivatives.
